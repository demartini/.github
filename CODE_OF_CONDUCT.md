# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free environment for everyone participating in our open source projects.

We expect contributors, maintainers, and community members to act professionally and respectfully, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual orientation.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that contribute to a positive community include:

- Showing empathy and kindness.
- Respecting different opinions, viewpoints, and experiences.
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback.
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its community.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- Sexualized language, imagery, attention, or advances.
- Trolling, insulting, derogatory, or harassing comments.
- Personal attacks or attacks based on protected characteristics.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported privately to:

**iolardemartini@gmail.com**

Reports will be reviewed as promptly and fairly as reasonably possible. Information related to a report will be handled with appropriate confidentiality.

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions that violate this Code of Conduct and may take appropriate corrective action.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to project spaces, including repositories, issue trackers, pull requests, discussions, and other official community channels.

It also applies when an individual is officially representing a project or community in public spaces.
